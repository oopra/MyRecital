Presentation

Picture-first puts the text at subtitle size along the bottom, turns off the emphasis colouring and the word-by-word reveal, and calms the camera — for reels where people on screen carry the story instead of the typography.

Narration

A narrator is what lets the words leave the screen: with a voice carrying the story, captions drop to ordinary subtitles and the picture gets the frame. Needs your own key — roughly 1–3¢ per scene. Each scene is re-timed to the length of its line.

Score

The score is synthesised from the scene moods — no audio files, and it re-writes itself when you re-cut the story. It is mixed into the recording.

Recording runs in real time with the sound — a 45-second reel takes 45 seconds. Keep this tab visible; a backgrounded tab throttles the frame loop.
